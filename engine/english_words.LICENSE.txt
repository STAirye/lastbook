engine/english_words.txt.gz
===========================

78,238 lower-case English words (plain a-z), one per line: every form of every word of the hunspell en_US
dictionary as Chromium ships it for spell checking (en-US-10-2.bdic), with the dictionary's affixes applied. Made
with tools/english_words.py from the copy of that file in a local Chrome install. engine/typos.py uses it so that an
ordinary English word ("keeled", "puking", "gasps") is not "corrected" into a word of the library.
